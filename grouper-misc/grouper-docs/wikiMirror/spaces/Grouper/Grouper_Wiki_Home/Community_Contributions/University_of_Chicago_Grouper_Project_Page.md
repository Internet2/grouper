Grouper at University of Chicago is being used to manage access to around 100 services, including wireless, LMS, Box.com, business objects, Google Apps, grants management system, two-factor authentication, Bronze and Silver Assurance, file sharing, web-based file storage, VOIP services, VPN, faculty class management applications, email routing, and student information system.

For info on Grouper and Box at University of Chicago, see [the email thread from January 2016](https://lists.internet2.edu/sympa/arc/grouper-dev/2016-01/msg00002.html)
