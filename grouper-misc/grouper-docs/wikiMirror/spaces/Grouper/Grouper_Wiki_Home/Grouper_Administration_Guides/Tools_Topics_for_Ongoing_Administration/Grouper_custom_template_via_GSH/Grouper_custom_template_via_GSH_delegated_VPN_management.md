This GSH template allows school and center VPN admins to manage their instance of a dept VPN

There are three GSH templates to make this work:

1. [Manage VPN (for department admins or central networking)](https://grouper.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/Grouper/pages/28554430/Grouper+custom+template+via+GSH+delegated+VPN+management+-+manage)
2. [Add / edit VPN instance (for central networking people only)](https://grouper.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/Grouper/pages/28555787/Grouper+custom+template+via+GSH+delegated+VPN+management+-+add)
3. [Delete VPN instance (for central networking people only)](https://grouper.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/Grouper/pages/28554486/Grouper+custom+template+via+GSH+delegated+VPN+management+-+delete)

Each VPN has 4 "roles"

1. Owner
  
  1. Can manage the lists of admins or owners. Attests the membership report. Can do anything admins can do.
2. Admin
  
  1. Can add / remove VPN employee users or guests
3. Employee user  
  
  
  1. Can use VPN until employment ends
4. Guest
  
  1. Can use VPN
