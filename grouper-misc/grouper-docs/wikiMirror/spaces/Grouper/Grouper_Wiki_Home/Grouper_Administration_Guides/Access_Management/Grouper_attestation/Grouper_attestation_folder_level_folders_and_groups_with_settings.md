To see all of the folders and groups that have settings (that you have access to see) in a certain folder, go to the folder, click More actions → Attestation → Attestation actions → Sub-folders and sub-groups with settings, or if in the global attestation screen, click "Attestation actions → Sub-folders and sub-groups with settings"
