- There have been some changes in IAM Landscape
- Want to ensure our training aligns with the needs of IT Professionals
- Hope to enhance training with particular focus on Grouper Training
- Input solicitation from Grouper team
- Have had success w Grouper Training
- We get some feedback on what is needed
- Many attendees say: we love the instructors, so knowledgeable , so much expertise
- Students like
  
  - hands on practices and self directed work
  - the VMs
  - having materials they can refer to in the future
- There is a varied set of attendees at Grouper Training
- Some beginners, some more advanced
- For new students, training can go too fast
- Hands on activities may not be relevant for the attendees’ role
- On Demand / Self Paced versus interaction w live instructors
