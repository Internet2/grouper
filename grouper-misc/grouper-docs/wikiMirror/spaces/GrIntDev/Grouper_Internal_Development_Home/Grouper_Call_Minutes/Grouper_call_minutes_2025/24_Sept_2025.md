### **Current Work**

**Subject Source Migration Process (from Zoom AI)**

The team discussed the process of migrating from an old subject source to a new data field subject source, with Chris explaining the need to disable the old source, create a new one with a different ID, and run a GSH script to test functionality before enabling the new source. They clarified that data field assignments require member records, which led to a discussion about the challenges of managing multiple subject IDs. The team also noted that while the data field subject source creates member records even for unassigned subjects, this was necessary to maintain data integrity and avoid synchronization issues.
