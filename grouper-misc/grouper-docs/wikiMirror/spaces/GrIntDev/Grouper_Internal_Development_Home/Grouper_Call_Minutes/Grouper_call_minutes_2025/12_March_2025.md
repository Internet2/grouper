**JEXL Script Security Progress (from Zoom AI)**

Vivek discussed the progress of implementing security around JEXL script usage while setting up loaders. He explained that the system will allow or restrict actions based on the permissions specified in the JEXL script.

Chris Hyzer clarified the roles, stating that 'readers' can analyze but not save, 'viewers' can analyze but not save, and 'updaters' can analyze and save.

Chris also mentioned the upcoming release that will allow admins to access the scripted group part of the loader based on their privileges.

Gail and Dusty shared their experiences with JEXL scripting, with Gail expressing uncertainty about the correct permission setup.

Shilen mentioned that Duke recently upgraded to v. 5.

Chris then discussed the current ABAC landscape at Penn, highlighting four rows configured.

**Setting Up Rows and Data Modeling from Zoom AI)**

Chris Hyzer discussed the complexities of setting up rows and the importance of doing it correctly. He mentioned the need for training to communicate these types of things. Chris also talked about the process of analyzing data elements from their warehouse and identifying common elements. He demonstrated how to model data fields and create ABAC for different roles such as students and instructors. Chris emphasized the need for clear documentation of each row representing a student or instructor in a course section.

**Rules and Attributes in Life Cycle (****from Zoom AI)**

Chris Hyzer discussed the potential for rules based on attributes and the need to address life cycles. Dusty inquired about the possibility of tying roles to attributes, which Chris agreed to consider. Gail raised concerns about the differences between versions 5.16 and 5.17, attributing them to the handling of null values and transitional issues. Chris suggested adding less than or greater than values to handle these issues. Gail also discussed the challenges of determining current and past terms in their data source, which Chris acknowledged as a different problem.

**Data Row Setup Challenges (****from Zoom AI)**

Chris Hyzer discussed the complexities of data row setup, particularly in relation to course sections and divisions. He explained the concept of a related course section and the challenges of communicating multi-valued cell attributes. Gail suggested sharing data solutions.   
Chris also demonstrated his use of views in data management, highlighting the complexity of translating data into Ts and Fs.  
 Dusty inquired about the roadmap for incremental loading, which Chris confirmed is on the roadmap.   
Shilen reported on a recent change to ensure that composites are not edited before they are created, and discussed potential changes to the history in fields.
