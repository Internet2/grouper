**Grouper Website Migration Challenges**

Chris Hyzer discussed issues with importing the Grouper website to Confluence, including problems with template macros and permalinks that have changed during the migration. The team identified that while the content was imported successfully, navigation and links were affected by the domain change.

Chris and Chad explored potential solutions, including moving to the cloud and manually managing user permissions, though specific implementation details were left unresolved.

**Confluence to Atlassian Migration Planning**

The team discussed migrating from their current Confluence setup to a new Atlassian environment, with Chris Hyzer noting that while attachments don't import, they should consider testing the free 25-user premium license during the transition period.

They explored licensing options including SCIM integration requirements and domain claiming processes, though Chris Hubing expressed reluctance about setting up additional domains due to email routing requirements.

The discussion concluded with plans to begin preparing the new environment while continuing experiments, with Chris Hubing taking the lead on wiki editing and preparing the new space for potential cutover.

**Grouper Group Import Modifications**

The team discussed recent modifications to the group import functionality in Grouper, where Vivek explained that users can now add multiple groups and group names/IDs through a file import feature with validation. Chris Hyzer inquired about a new "skip if target group exists" provisioning option, with Carey and Chad expressing concerns about potential confusion and diagnostic challenges with this feature. The team agreed to follow up with Oregon to clarify their specific requirements for the provisioning behavior.

**UI Updates and Jira Progress**

The team discussed updates on various Jiras and UI improvements. Chris Hyzer demonstrated recent changes including a fixed sync button in V7 and updates to the subject source filter description. The group agreed to make the reports button available in non-admin sections to improve user navigation, though they noted this would require batched performance optimization. Vivek mentioned working on several smaller Jiras but did not have additional updates to share.

**Technical Issues**

- - - The team discussed several technical issues and updates.
    - Chad and Gail identified problems with the Michigan Okta provisioner taking over 24 hours to complete due to long data retrieval times, with no progress logging visible.
    - Chris Hyzer agreed to add progress logging to help debug these issues.
    - Shilen reported completing work on dropping unused indexes from the grouper temp changelog and implementing incremental SQL loader improvements with new sync capabilities.
    - Chris Hyzer demonstrated new ABAC visualization improvements that better format long scripts and group affiliations, though the team discussed potential improvements to the plus/or notation in the visualization.
    - The conversation ended with Chris Hyzer presenting new write-back capabilities for provisioners and performance improvements in version 7.2, including certificate authentication support for Entra.
