**Summary**

The Grouper team discussed the migration from interpreted Groovy scripts to compiled Java templates. Chris presented a comprehensive analysis of the pros and cons of moving to compiled Java, highlighting performance improvements, better error handling, and improved debugging capabilities, but acknowledged concerns about migration complexity and additional work required from users. The team debated whether to implement this change in version 9 or delay it, with Chad expressing concerns about forcing users to upgrade while Shillen and others supporting the move for better consistency. Additionally, the team reviewed various completed and ongoing development items including performance graphs for daemon logs, subject source filtering improvements, and ABAC script enhancements.   
Chris also demonstrated a new Git-based wiki system that will help maintain versioned documentation alongside the codebase, with AI integration for better search and navigation capabilities.
