**Call Summary (from Zoom AI)**

- The team held a development meeting focused on reviewing recent work and discussing ongoing issues. Vivek presented updates on several completed JIRAs, including audit log exports, Okta provisioning improvements, and loader configuration changes.
- Shilen reported on fixes for diagnostics and comparison screens related to data field subject sources.
- The team discussed performance issues with the incremental loader, particularly regarding group creation conflicts when groups are deleted and recreated with the same name but different ID paths.
- Chris Hyzer and Chad debated database indexing strategies for membership queries, with Chad advocating for removing sort string indexes while Chris argued for their importance.
- The meeting also covered accessibility compliance discussions, with plans to engage either an internal accessibility unit or external contractors to review and improve Grouper's accessibility.
- Several other technical issues were discussed including MCP improvements, data provider performance optimizations, and various bug fixes across different components of the system.
