| Answer to prompt “What is Internet2 Grouper” from University of Michigan LLM. Might be a copy of existing text.    Internet2 Grouper is an open-source group management toolkit designed for higher education institutions to manage group memberships, roles, and access control in a secure and efficient manner. It is part of the Internet2 Middleware Initiative and aims to provide a robust framework for identity and access management (IAM).    **Key Features of Internet2 Grouper:**    - Group Management: - Access Control: - Integration: - Delegated Administration: - Audit and Compliance: - Provisioning: - Web and Command-Line Interfaces:  Grouper plays a critical role in managing digital identities and streamlining the access management process across various services and applications, making it a valuable tool for universities and research institutions. |
