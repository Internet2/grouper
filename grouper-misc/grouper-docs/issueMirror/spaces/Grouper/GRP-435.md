# GRP-435  Paging not working for effective membership lists where one or more members are members by more than one path

Clicking next page for membership list showed first and second pages of results together

## Comments

### Gary Brown - 2010-05-17T10:06:25.032+0000

The initial retrieval of memberships may include duplicate members and is processed to produce a new, potentially smaller list. The size of the original list rather than the de-duplicated list was being used incorrectly leading to the paging error
