# GRP-113  UI bug where group is not listed after creating a new group

1.  Click on the Create Group button.
2.  Enter a group id and name and click "Add Members".
3.  Click on one of the available groups (without doing a search).
4.  Click "Assign Privileges".
5.  Click "Assign Privileges".
6.  Click "Back to group summary".
7.  Under "Current location is:  ", click on the parent stem.

The list of available groups does not contain the group just created.  If you click the "Explore" link, the list will refresh and include the new group.
