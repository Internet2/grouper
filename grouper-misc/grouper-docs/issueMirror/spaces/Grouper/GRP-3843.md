## Comments

### Scott Cantor - 2022-02-22T17:34:02.627+0000

As an aside, it's really bad logging practice to log through utility classes because you lose the class/line number information. The whole value of diagnostic logging by class is to expose that detail for debugging. You could of course pass them in as parameters to the logging method, but you're just redoing what good logging libraries (and even bad ones like log4j) do for you.
