### bert - 2018-10-03T21:14:21.676+0000

As a RuntimeException, this isn't caught very close to the action, however, I'll look to see if I can make improvements in this particular case. 

For the general problem... Instead of making every log include contextual information, PSPNG uses [MDCs|https://logging.apache.org/log4j/1.2/apidocs/org/apache/log4j/MDC.html] to augment logs in a more scalable/consistent way

We talked about this years ago, but it never went beyond pspng. What do you think about adding those %X's to the standard log4j files?

log4j.appender.grouper_error.layout.ConversionPattern  = %d\{ISO8601}: [%t] %-5p [%c\{1}] %X\{why}%X\{who}%X\{what}%X\{step} - %x - %m%n
