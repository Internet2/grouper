# GRP-5604  In subject view, annotate attributes that are identifiers

This would help slightly with the setup of loader jobs and provisioners. The subject view shows the user's attributes as is. But to know which are identifiers, and which is identifier0, identifier1, etc., you need to look at how the subject source is configured. Having something on the subject page as well would save that step. It could be something on the attributes themselves, or in a separate section after the attributes.
