### John Gasper III - 2020-05-07T21:08:22.847+0000

There is definitely precedence for doing something like this in images/containers where it make sense to init a database:
 * [https://hub.docker.com/_/mysql] (see Initializing a fresh instance)
 * [https://hub.docker.com/_/postgres] (see Initialization scripts)
 * [https://hub.docker.com/_/mongo|https://hub.docker.com/_/mongo?] (see MONGO_INITDB_DATABASE)

In other (non-data initializing) context, these scripts are really just helpers for orgs/individuals that do not have a DevOps (i.e. git/build/publish environment). While I'm a proponent of trying to get to a distro-less image (which would not have bash, etc), these scripts can be helpful for deployers that want to tweak/customize the container without building their own image to make such changes.
