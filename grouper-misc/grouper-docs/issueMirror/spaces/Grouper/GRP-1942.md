My commit was after his email and I hadn’t created a patch for it because patches were backlogged.   In the end, you can’t have the base dn in both grouper-loader.properties and subject.properties.  I had opted to remove it from subject.base.properties (and add an explanation there) only because I’m assuming many people are already using the base dn with grouper-loader.properties since it has always been documented that way (in the properties file and on the wiki).  So for those people, changing it could be painful since they’d have to go through all of their loader jobs.  But again, either way is fine and it’s probably just important to make sure it’s documented (which it is now in subject.base.properties at least).
