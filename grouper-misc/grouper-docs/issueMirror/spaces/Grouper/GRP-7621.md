# GRP-7621  Upgrade jackson to 2.22.4 for security

The 7.10.0 image ships jackson-databind 2.22.3 and jackson-dataformat-cbor 2.17.2. The cbor jar comes in through aws-java-sdk-core and was not aligned to jackson.version, because the grouper-container poms do not inherit grouper-parent and did not import the jackson BOM. Image scanners flag:

- jackson-databind 2.22.3: CVE-2026-110879 (fixed in 2.22.4, jackson-databind #6287)
- jackson-dataformat-cbor 2.17.2: CVE-2026-68495 (fixed in 2.22.2, #725) and CVE-2026-106554 (fixed in 2.22.4, #821)

Change: jackson.version 2.22.3 to 2.22.4 in grouper-parent/pom.xml, and import jackson-bom ${jackson.version} in the dependency management of the three grouper-container poms (like the netty BOM, GRP-7504), so every jackson jar in the image is 2.22.4.
