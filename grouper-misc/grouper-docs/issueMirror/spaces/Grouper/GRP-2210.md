# GRP-2210  PSPNG - Caching: Share more information between Full & Incremental Syncs

PSPNG's Full and Incremental processing sometimes need different information about the Target-LDAP groups, but need the same information about the other three items it caches: GrouperGroups, GrouperSubjects and Target-LDAP Users.

Therefore, Full and Incremental processes should share those three caches and maintain their own Target-Group caches.
