# GRP-7567  Group summary shows empty Direct group members list when group has 5 or more direct group members

On the group Summary tab, the Memberships section shows the label "Direct group members:" with nothing after it when the group has exactly 5 or more direct group members.

Seen in the Grouper training (lesson 01): ref:student:students has 4 class year groups plus one graduated year group as direct members. Summary shows 1521 members, 1516 non-group members, 5 direct members, and then an empty "Direct group members:" line.

Cause: UiV2Group.java (group summary) counts direct g:gsa members, and only loads the list of those groups when the count is less than 5. groupSummaryMoreMemberships.jsp shows the "Direct group members:" line whenever the count is greater than 0, so for 5 or more the label shows with an empty list.

Fix: when the list of groups is not loaded (5 or more), show the count in the same style as the lines above it, e.g. "5 direct group members". When fewer than 5, keep the list of group links. New text key groupSummaryPageMembershipsDirectGroupMembersCount.
