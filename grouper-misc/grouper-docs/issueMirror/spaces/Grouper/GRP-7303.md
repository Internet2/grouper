# GRP-7303  UI accessibility: group summary heading hierarchy skipped a level (h1 to h3)

On the group screen the document outline jumped from the group h1 straight to h3 with no h2 (SC 1.3.1, 2.4.6). The two section headings under the group title - Group summary and Fields, both in groupSummary.jsp - were h3.

Fix: promoted those two headings from h3 to h2 so heading levels increase by one step (h1 to h2 to h2). The visible size is driven by the inner p.lead, so the change is style-neutral. Verified live: the group screen outline is now h1 then h2 then h2, one h1, zero skips. Folder and subject summaries were checked and have only an h1 (no section-heading skip), so they needed no change. The Add-members modal title is left as h3 by design - it is a dialog title (role=dialog + aria-labelledby) and is not part of the page outline in the normal closed state.
