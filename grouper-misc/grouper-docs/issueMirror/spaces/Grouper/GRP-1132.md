Hi,
 
I have a problem with the “grouperLoaderGroupsLike” attribute of my loader jobs. The loader group is executed correctly and the groups to be deleted are also found. But if they are members of another group they are not deleted. The daily report is full of error messages like
 
subjobFor_XXXXXXX
status:            WARNING, started: 2015-04-23 14:05:32.065 (62ms)
ins/upd/del/tot:   0/0/0/0
error:             Not deleting group: XXXXXXX since used in 1 immediate memberships
 
All of these groups are empty but are members of other groups. I would expect that they are deleted nevertheless, as well as you can delete them in the UI although they are members anywhere else.
Now the database is full with groups that should have been deleted already. Usdu does not help, because the groups do still exist.
