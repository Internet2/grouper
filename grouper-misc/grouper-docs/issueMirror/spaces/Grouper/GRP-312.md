# GRP-312  Group deletions do not delete naming privileges where the group is the member

When a group is deleted, if that group has naming privileges to stems, those memberships are not deleted.  This will be fixed by adding revokeAllPrivilegesForSubject(Subject) to the access and naming adapters.
