# GRP-3396  reports on groups cannot be retrieved from email by non admins

Liam Hoekenga Apr 16th at 10:58 AM
I’m having an issue with report retrieval. I added a viewers group to the report configuration. Everyone in the viewers group gets email about the report. Top level grouper admins (the members of etc:sysadmingroup ) can follow the link in the email to the report summary. The members of the viewers group get the “There was an error. Click here to start over.” page.
