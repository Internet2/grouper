Carey Black [3 hours ago]
I would strongly suggest that this is not needed.

As a general rule, only grant privileges through groups. Then you can do all of the "group member management" ( rules, group math, etc...) outside of the "privileges on objects" layer.

Carey Black [2 hours ago]
I doubt that I fully understand all of your challenges here.

However, I look at the Access Management (AM) world this way.

Grouper is an AM tool.
Grouper is an application too.

So Grouper should manage Access Control Policies ( ACP's) that drive access (and privileges) to itself. And yes, those ACP's can use SOR reference groups just like every ACP can.

If your design does not allow for your Grouper ACP's to be reused (across groups/objects, etc..) then that is your choice and it means many more groups. But that is an AM design choice. (And one that could be needed, or hostel to "good management practices" too.) However, I suspect that the "rules/logic" that would need to be assigned to "Privileges" would be even more complex than using the design of Grouper ACP's on Grouper objects. ( YMMV, HTH )
