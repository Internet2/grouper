# GRP-7335  Remove cookies if not useful: _grouper_loggedOut, etc.

When an institution wants to control SameSite settings on cookies, the ones Grouper hardcodes can’t be configured without modifying the source code. Cookies like _grouper_loggedOut are ancient and are likely not useful to keep around.
