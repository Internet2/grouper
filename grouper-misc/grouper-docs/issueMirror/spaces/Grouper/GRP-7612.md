# GRP-7612  SCIM: deprecate the Grouper specific /Memberships endpoint, accept the standard count parameter

The SCIM /Memberships endpoint is a Grouper specific resource (from the TIER SCIM API), not standard SCIM. Once Group responses can omit members (GRP-7609), PATCH /Groups adds and removes members (GRP-7610), and GET /Users pages the members of a group (GRP-7611), the standard endpoints cover what most clients need.

Change: mark /Memberships deprecated in the release notes and the SCIM documentation, and ask on the Grouper list who uses it (in particular the membership type and the enabled / disabled times, which standard SCIM members do not carry). Meanwhile accept the standard count parameter on GET /Memberships in addition to itemsPerPage. Decide in a later release whether to remove it or keep a read-only version. Do not deprecate before GRP-7610 and GRP-7611 are done.
