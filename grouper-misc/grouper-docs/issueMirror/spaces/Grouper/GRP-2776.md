### Chris Hyzer - 2020-05-08T21:00:04.162+0000

Current env vars will work but are deprecated and will be removed in the future.  Please change your references to use the new env vars.  The namespace for Grouper container env vars is GROUPER prefix going forward

GROUPER_RUN_SHIB_SP was RUN_SHIB_SP
GROUPER_RUN_TOMEE was RUN_TOMEE
GROUPER_RUN_HSQLDB was RUN_HSQLDB
GROUPER_SELF_SIGNED_CERT was SELF_SIGNED_CERT
