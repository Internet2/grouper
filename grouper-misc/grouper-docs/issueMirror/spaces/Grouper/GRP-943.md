# GRP-943  Feature Request: Contact Group-or-Folder Admin(s)

While we try to delegate out groups administration as much as possible at uchicago, we still run into problems where a) someone needs a membership or some other change made on a group but b) they have no idea who to contact to have that change made.  This leads to support requests where we often end up trying to route them to the right delegated admin/admins.

What would be useful is if there was either a "Contact Admins" button and/or attribute displayed in the UI that contained contact information for the group/folder.  If there was a way for us to make this mandatory for at least certain folder structures, even better.
