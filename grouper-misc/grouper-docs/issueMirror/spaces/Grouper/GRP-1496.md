# GRP-1496  Membership GracePeriods: Associate with nested-group membership

This issue is requesting that Grouper support an attribute (associated with the membership of a group (eg, GroupR) within another group (eg, GroupS)) which defines how long subjects stay in GroupS after they are removed from GroupR.

Georgia Tech's use case for this is where GroupR is a reference group (eg, Students), and GroupS is a service group (eg, Office365-users). We want to give a former student a few weeks to keep Office365 after they leave... so we can warn them they are going to lose service and so they can either complain (and get a mistake fixed or get the service granted somehow) or migrate their data.

Our hooks implementation of this traps on subjects being removed from GroupR, looks to see if that group is nested in another group with this attribute, and then adds the subjects (with a membership end-date) to a GroupS-GracePeriod group (which, of course, is also a member of GroupS).

We intend for our notification system to look at *-GracePeriod memberships and notify members of the upcoming changes.
