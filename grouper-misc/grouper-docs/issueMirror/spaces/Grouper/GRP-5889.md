## Comments

### Bert Bee-Lindgren - 2025-02-24T19:59:28.487+0000

GT is going to proceed as if this is a placeholder issue for Incremental ABAC/JEXL....

We have thousands of JEXL-loaded groups that essentially implement flexible group math (without the intermediate groups). ((A+B) & (C+D)) - (E+F))  A-allow-rules, B-allow-adhoc, C-global eligible, D-SvcEligible, E-DenyAll, F-DenySvc.

With 5.13.5, the JEXL loader is taking 12+ hours to refresh them, and our customers have gotten unlucky and seen ~24 hours for a AllowAdhoc membership to propagate to the Policy Group result.

We've seen references to db-performance improvements in a more recent version, and we'll get there eventually in the hopes that that gets things down to a 2-4hr cycle. However, we're commenting here because Incremental ABAC updates is probably our only chance to get things to the 5-30 minutes that would make such a difference.
