# GRP-2529  Describe responsibilities of data owners, policy managers, etc

acamp 2019 feedback

Who is the right person to design acc mgmt policy for a service? Service owner? IAM staff?; No one has expertise on access / authorization management and data model; Leaving it to service owner will lead to proliferation of bad data and practices.

Describe the role and responsibility of “data owner”, the person who has the authority to define a population, _and_ differentiate that to the role of “application owner”

Define organizational roles: data owners, iam analysts (ref data expertise), app owners, data owners (see above)
