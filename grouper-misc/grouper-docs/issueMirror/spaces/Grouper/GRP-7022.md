# GRP-7022  Document guidance to avoid policy groups that nest hundreds of sub-groups; recommend ABAC or custom loader instead

* Sites have hit performance pathologies (slow {{{}sqlCacheFullSync{}}}, membership resolution, provisioning) on policy groups built by nesting hundreds/thousands of sub-groups. UCLA's case: 74 cache_groups have >1000 active {{grouper_pit_group_set}} rows; populator can't finish on them.
 * Structural cause: every nesting adds permanent {{grouper_group_set}} rows (and PIT history) that compound with depth and breadth. Anything that walks flattened membership pays the fan-out.
 * Add wiki guidance covering:
 ** Cost of nesting and accumulating PIT history.
 ** Symptoms: stuck populator on one group, slow provisioner full-syncs, slow change-log catchup.
 ** Soft limit: avoid >~100 sub-groups in one policy group; hundreds is a warning, thousands is broken.
 ** Alternatives:
 ### *ABAC scripted group* — JEXL over subject attributes, no nesting fan-out.
 ### *Custom loader (SQL/LDAP)* — materialize directly from source, single nesting depth.
 ** Example: instead of nesting hundreds of org sub-groups, use ABAC checking "subject's org ∈ \{list}" or a SQL loader from the org system.
 * Pages: policy/composite groups (Drupal), Deployment Guide scaling section, ABAC page, Grouper Loader page, {{sqlCacheFullSync}} page (link to GRP-XXXX threshold Jira as the operational fallback).
 * Docs only, no code. Sentence-case headers.
