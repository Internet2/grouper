# GRP-1497  Membership Conditions: Subjects keep membership while they remain in another Group

This issue is requesting support for membership conditions that define how long a (non-group) subject would remain in another group. 

For example, let's say that Dr Jennifer is a Electrical Engineering professor, but is also a collaborator with the Biology department. We want the Biology department to be able to add Dr Jennifer to the BiologyIntranet-faculty group, but the Biology department wants her membership to go away when she leaves the EE department. This would be accomplished by associating the EE-Faculty group as a condition on her BiologyIntranet-Faculty membership.

Our hook implements this by trapping membership removals and removing any other memberships that are conditional on the first.

We are happy to share our hook code for this.
