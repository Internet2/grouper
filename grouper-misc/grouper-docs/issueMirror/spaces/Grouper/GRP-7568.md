# GRP-7568  Group summary non-group members count double counts subjects with multiple membership paths

On the group Summary tab, the "non-group members" count is a count of memberships, not of unique members. A subject who is a member through two paths (e.g. directly and through a member group, or through two member groups) is counted twice.

Seen in the Grouper training (lesson 02): ref:student:students shows 1584 members and 1576 non-group members. jmejia is in the group through two paths (a class year group and ref:student:transfer_student) and is counted twice.

Code: UiV2Group.java group summary, the "get not-group members count" block runs MembershipFinder with queryOptions.retrieveCount(true) and assignSources(non-group sources). The count is over membership rows, so each path is a row.

Fix: count distinct members (distinct member id) for the non-group members count. The total members count just below it uses the same pattern and likely has the same problem, so check and fix that too. Direct counts are not affected since a subject has at most one immediate membership.
