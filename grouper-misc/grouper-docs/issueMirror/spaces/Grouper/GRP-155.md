# GRP-155  normalize the grouper_fields table

The foreign keys to grouper_fields from attributes and memberships are driven by name and name/type.  These relationships require two unique constraints on grouper_fields (name, and name/type) [for dbs such as oracle and postgres].  This is inconvenient for several reasons, but the most important is ddlutils doesnt support them.  We discussed and I will make these key off of uuid instead...
