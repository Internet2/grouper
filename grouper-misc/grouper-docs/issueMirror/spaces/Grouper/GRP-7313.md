# GRP-7313  UI accessibility: hide decorative Font Awesome icons from assistive technology (WCAG 1.1.1)

Accessibility action-plan item 10. Font Awesome glyphs in the Grouper v2 UI are decorative and sit in controls that already carry a name, but the glyph elements were exposed to screen readers (as private-use characters). Added aria-hidden="true" to the decorative icons and gave the two icon-only remove-group buttons an accessible name. WCAG 1.1.1 Non-text Content.

*What changed:*

* 189 literal <i class="fa"> icons across ~80 JSPs got aria-hidden.
* 38 icon-bearing text templates (guiGroupShort, guiStemLink, guiSubjectShort, membershipTrace*, etc.) in grouper.textNg.en.us.base.properties - this also covers the jstree/dojo folder-tree node icons, which are built from those templates.
* 4 config-driven subject icons (grouperUi.screenSubjectIcon2.screenHtmlEl.*) in grouper-ui-ng.base.properties.
* 11 breadcrumb fa-angle-right dividers plus a fallback fa-user in GuiObjectBase.java / GrouperUiUtils.java.
* The two nameless icon-only remove-group buttons (groupImportExtraGroups.jsp, inviteExtraGroups.jsp) got role=button + aria-label="Remove group <name>", matching their remove-subject siblings.

Verified live: all 21 Font Awesome icons on the home page (static, template-driven entity/tree, and config-driven subject icons) are aria-hidden, with 0 exposed, while the containing controls keep their accessible names.
