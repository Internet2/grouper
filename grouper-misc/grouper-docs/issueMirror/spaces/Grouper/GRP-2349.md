# GRP-2349  Have the FullSync and the Incremental provisioners share the list of ProvisionedGroups

We are on P11.

When initialize increamental provisioner and fullsync provisioner, they both load all the provisioning groups. To get better performance, they can share the provisioning group list.

We have 77k provisioning groups, it take 40~50 mins for initialize both provisioner.
