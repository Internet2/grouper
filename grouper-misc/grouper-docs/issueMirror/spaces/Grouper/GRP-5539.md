### Daniel Fisher - 2024-07-27T13:58:13.544+0000

[~raymond.walker@example.com] in general you want to avoid referrals if you can, but you definitely want to avoid both paging and referrals. I submitted a patch that will hopefully resolve your issue, but it's worth noting that this code won't handle a referral that is sent in the middle of a paged result set. Microsoft talks about that here:

 [LDAP Paged Queries with subordinate referrals are not chased properly|https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/windows-server/active-directory/ldap-paged-queries-subordinate-referrals-not-chased]

#1 and #2 are the most common solutions, but #4 could be implemented.
