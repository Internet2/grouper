# GRP-7421  AssetSonar provisioner does not report progress during paged member reads

A full sync of the AssetSonar provisioner (GRP-7420) pages through every active member and then every inactive member, 25 per page (about 90 seconds for a 4,400 member tenant), with no live progress in the job message. Report the target progress label per page, e.g. retrieving active members from target: page 12 of 140, 300 so far, the same way the CCure and Datadog provisioners do (GRP-7154, GRP-7181).
